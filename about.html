About Tham Wilson 🌿

Working Hours

The worker begins his duties at 8:00 a.m. and completes his work at 5:00 p.m., ensuring the cave is ready for visitors throughout the day.

Daily Preparation

Before visitors arrive, he inspects the cave pathways, checks lighting, and ensures that all areas are clean and safe.

Memorable Experience

He enjoys meeting tourists from various countries, including Thailand, Indonesia, China, and Europe.

Passion

Working close to nature allows him to appreciate the beauty of Gua Kelam while interacting with people from different backgrounds.

Pride & Purpose

He takes pride in his role as he contributes to preserving one of the most beautiful natural attractions in Perlis.
