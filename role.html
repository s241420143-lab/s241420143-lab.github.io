Role & Contribution 🌿

Responsibilities

The worker maintains the cave environment by checking pathways, ensuring cleanliness, and monitoring lighting conditions.

Assisting Visitors

He guides tourists, provides information, and ensures that visitors follow safety rules while exploring the cave.

Challenges

Managing large crowds and ensuring safety during rainy weather can be difficult due to slippery surfaces.

Contribution

His work helps preserve Gua Kelam’s natural beauty and supports sustainable tourism for future generations.
